# Chore Heroes — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Chore Heroes is a chore chart for families with children aged 6 to 9. We built it so that
**nothing about your family ever leaves your device.**

## The short version

- **We collect no data.** Not your name, not your children's names, not how the app is used.
- **No accounts, no login, no sign-up.**
- **No ads. No analytics. No tracking.** There are no third-party advertising, analytics,
  crash-reporting or tracking SDKs in the app.
- **No internet connection.** The app never connects to a server. On Android, the published
  app does not even request the Internet permission.
- **Everything is stored only on your device**, inside the app's private storage.
- **No links out of the app in the children's area**, no in-app purchases, no chat, no
  sharing with other users.

## What the app stores, and where

All of this is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your iPad or Android
device. We (the developer) cannot see it.

| Information                                                               | Why it is stored                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Child profile: first name or nickname, age (6–9), chosen avatar and color | To show each child their own jobs and stars      |
| Jobs, rewards and schedules you create                                    | To show the right jobs each day                  |
| Job check-offs, approvals, reward requests and notes like "Nice job!"     | History, star balance and progress               |
| Star history (each star earned or spent, with a reason)                   | The star balance is calculated from this history |
| Badges and streaks                                                        | Progress and celebrations                        |
| Settings (sound, read-aloud, reduce motion, font, reminder time)          | Your preferences                                 |
| Parent PIN — stored only as a salted one-way hash, never in plain text    | To keep the grown-up area private                |

We recommend using a first name or nickname for each child.

## Parental controls

- The grown-up area (jobs, rewards, approvals, dashboards) is protected by a **4-digit parent
  PIN**, with a lockout after repeated wrong attempts. It locks automatically after a few
  minutes of inactivity and whenever the app is closed.
- **Settings and data export** also require a **parental gate** (a multiplication question
  most young children cannot answer).

## Permissions

| Permission          | Used for                                                                                                                 | Required? |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- |
| Notifications       | An optional daily reminder that you turn on in Settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device; no push service is used. | No        |
| Vibration / haptics | Gentle feedback when a job is done (follows the Sound setting)                                                           | No        |

The app does **not** use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, advertising
identifier, or any account. Read-aloud uses the device's built-in text-to-speech voice, which
runs on the device.

## Exporting and deleting your data

- **Export:** In Settings (after the parental gate), _Export data_ creates a file and opens
  your device's share sheet. You decide where it goes (Files, AirDrop, e-mail…). The export
  never includes your PIN. Once you share it, that copy is under your control.
- **Delete:** _Settings → Erase all data_ permanently deletes everything. Uninstalling the
  app also deletes all of its data. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app, so data
  isn't copied to the cloud.

## Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K)

Because the app collects no personal information and transmits nothing, the developer does
not receive, store or share any information from children. There is nothing for us to
disclose, sell or delete on our side — all data is in your hands, on your device.

## App store compliance notes

- **Apple Kids Category:** no third-party analytics or advertising, no external links or
  purchase opportunities in the children's experience, parental gate before settings and
  data export, App Privacy label: _Data Not Collected_, privacy manifest declares no tracking
  and no collected data types.
- **Google Play Families Policy:** no ads or ad SDKs, no collection or transmission of
  personal or device information (including the advertising ID, which is blocked), no
  location, Data safety form: _No data collected, no data shared_.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with an app update and this page will
show the new date. We will never start collecting data from children.
